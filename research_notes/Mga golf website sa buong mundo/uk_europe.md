# Top UK, Ireland & Continental Europe Golf Clubs/Resorts: Online Services for Players (late 2026)

Clubs covered: St Andrews Links (Scotland), Gleneagles (Scotland), Adare Manor (Ireland), Costa Navarino (Greece), PGA Catalunya, which now trades as **Camiral** (Spain). No substitutions were needed. PGA Catalunya's old URL (pgacatalunya.com/en/golf) now returns a 301 redirect to camiral.com, and the site says "PGA Catalunya is now Camiral", part of Quinta do Lago Resorts — [Camiral](https://www.camiral.com/en).

Research date: 5 Nov 2026. Some official pages (St Andrews ballot and Links Ticketholder pages) returned only navigation to the fetch tool, so St Andrews booking rules come from the App Store listing and 2026 golf media. Those sources are flagged below.

## Tee-time booking (engines, ballots, windows, handicaps, deposits, caddies/buggies, pricing)

### Takeaway
The five sites cover the full range. St Andrews runs a multi-channel lottery (daily ballot, singles draw, and a lottery-allocated guaranteed package). Gleneagles and Costa Navarino use white-label third-party engines (Chronogolf, Golfmanager). Adare Manor has no online booking at all: residents only, by enquiry. Clear per-season price tables, a resident-vs-visitor price split, and caddie/cart add-on prices are standard. None of the sites publishes dynamic pricing. Published cancellation and deposit policies were hard to find.

### Cited Findings
**St Andrews Links**
- Daily Old Course ballot: entries close at 2pm two days before play (enter Tuesday for Thursday). The Old Course is closed Sundays, so there is no Sunday ballot. Groups are 2–4 golfers; solo golfers cannot enter. Each golfer gives name, home club and handicap. Maximum handicap is 36, and proof may be requested. Entry is free, and unsuccessful entrants lose nothing. Results come out later that afternoon. Article dated 16 Sep 2026 — [Top100 Golf Courses](https://www.top100golfcourses.com/golf-travel/how-the-st-andrews-old-course-ballot-actually-works-everything-you-need-to-know)
- Golf365 (8 Mar 2026) says ballot entries can be made "online, by phone, or at a clubhouse" before 2pm two days ahead — [Golf365](https://www.golf365.com/news/how-to-get-tee-time-st-andrews). Top100 mentions only online entry (standrews.com/old-course-ballot) — [Top100](https://www.top100golfcourses.com/golf-travel/how-the-st-andrews-old-course-ballot-actually-works-everything-you-need-to-know). The official app also lets you "Enter the Old Course Ballot" — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/st-andrews-links-home-of-golf/id6670563713)
- Handicap limits conflict across secondary sources: 36 for all, or 24 men / 36 women — [WebSearch summary of golfforkings / Welcome to Fife results](https://www.welcometofife.com/golf-news-article/6-ways-to-book-the-old-course). Top100 (Sep 2026) says 36 — [Top100](https://www.top100golfcourses.com/golf-travel/how-the-st-andrews-old-course-ballot-actually-works-everything-you-need-to-know). Not verified on the official site.
- Singles Daily Draw (introduced March 2024, replacing the overnight singles queue): enter in person 9am–5pm at the Old Pavilion or the Links Clubhouse. The draw is held at 5pm the day before play, winners are notified by **email and SMS**, and they are slotted into groups with a free space — [Top100](https://www.top100golfcourses.com/golf-travel/how-the-st-andrews-old-course-ballot-actually-works-everything-you-need-to-know). Golf365 says players are notified around 7pm — [Golf365](https://www.golf365.com/news/how-to-get-tee-time-st-andrews). The times conflict.
- Advance reservations: the annual process typically opens at the start of September for the following year, for groups of 2–8 golfers — [Top100](https://www.top100golfcourses.com/golf-travel/how-the-st-andrews-old-course-ballot-actually-works-everything-you-need-to-know)
- Guaranteed "Swilcan Package", booked via standrews.com: two rounds on consecutive days, one on the Old Course. £700 high season, £530 low season. Maximum 4 golfers per application, opened Sep 2025. Includes a £65 F&B credit, £25 retail credit and free range balls. Allocation is **by lottery** — [Golf365](https://www.golf365.com/news/how-to-get-tee-time-st-andrews)
- 2026 Old Course green fee: £355 high season (21 Apr–18 Oct 2026), £250 late Oct, £180 from 1 Nov 2026 to 31 Mar 2027, up from £340 in 2025 — [Golf Monthly via search](https://www.golfmonthly.com/news/how-much-it-will-cost-you-to-play-all-3-alfred-dunhill-links-championship-courses-in-2026-and-2027); [Golf365](https://www.golf365.com/news/how-to-get-tee-time-st-andrews)
- Local/member-type access: the St Andrews Links Ticket for North East Fife residents and term-time university students cost £386 (2025) and covers all seven courses — [Golf365](https://www.golf365.com/news/how-to-get-tee-time-st-andrews). In-app "Tee Time Booking" is "currently available to Links Ticketholders only" — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/st-andrews-links-home-of-golf/id6670563713)
- "Authorised Provider" scheme: licensed tour operators sell high-season tee times and display an "Authorised Provider" logo. Their prices are not published — [Golf365](https://www.golf365.com/news/how-to-get-tee-time-st-andrews)
- "The Drive" access initiative: Golf365 reports 179 tee times from May to October with 44 golfers playing the Old Course for £42.50 — [Golf365](https://www.golf365.com/news/how-to-get-tee-time-st-andrews). Details are thin; see Gaps.
- Caddie fee: roughly £75–80 per bag in 2026, per a secondary source only — [golfforkings](https://golfforkings.pages.dev/guides/green-fees-st-andrews/). Not verified on the official site.

**Gleneagles**
- Online booking through a **Chronogolf** widget ("Book Now") or by phone on 01764 662231 — [Gleneagles Golf](https://gleneagles.com/golf/); [Green fees](https://gleneagles.com/golf/green-fees/)
- Month-by-month 2026/27 green-fee tables with separate Hotel Guest and Visitor rates, a per-round and a per-day price, plus Wee Course and Junior columns. Examples: Jul '27 round £465 guest / £500 visitor; Dec '26 round £100 guest / £115 visitor. Day ticket Jul '27: £650 guest / £700 visitor — [Gleneagles green fees](https://gleneagles.com/golf/green-fees/)
- Rules: day tickets mean two rounds in one day, subject to availability, and golfers may be paired. **All visitor golf must be paid at booking**. Junior rates cover ages 17 and under playing with two paying adults. No handicap certificate is required — [Green fees](https://gleneagles.com/golf/green-fees/); [Golf](https://gleneagles.com/golf/)
- Carts cost £60 and are allowed on the PGA Centenary only, unless you have a medical exemption, in which case a caddie is also required on the King's and Queen's. Group caddie is £80/player ("From £91.50" also shown); a forecaddie for four golfers is £122 — [Green fees](https://gleneagles.com/golf/green-fees/)
- Bundled offers: "Tee Time offer" (4 players, Sun–Thu, Mar–Oct, from £160pp) and "Tee It Up" (one night plus one championship round) — [Green fees](https://gleneagles.com/golf/green-fees/)

**Adare Manor**
- No online tee-time engine. Booking goes through the golf/reservations team and a web "Golf enquiry" form (adaremanor.com/golf/enquiry/). Priority goes to resort guests; non-resident availability is limited, and "advance enquiry is essential" — [Adare Manor Golf](https://www.adaremanor.com/golf/)
- 2026 green fees page lists only a **Resident rate of €550 pp** (26 May–18 Oct 2026). Non-resident is "N/A". Closed Mondays except bank holidays, and closed Jan–Apr and from 19 Oct — [Adare green fees](https://www.adaremanor.com/golf/our-course/green-fees/)
- **Caddie mandatory** for all golfers (€120). No double-bagging; a forecaddie is offered for two golfers sharing a drive cart. Cart €70 (includes GPS, water, towels), electric trolley €25, pull trolley €15, club hire €75 (Titleist/TaylorMade plus 3 crested balls), range balls €15, 60-minute lesson €105 — [Adare green fees](https://www.adaremanor.com/golf/our-course/green-fees/)
- A 2023 guide gave a €95 visitor caddie fee, so older third-party data is out of date — [golfforkings via search](https://golfforkings.pages.dev/guides/how-to-play-adare-manor/)

**Costa Navarino**
- Online tee times run on a white-label **Golfmanager** engine at costanavarino.golfmanager.com/consumer/ebookings, plus phone (+30 27230 97000) — [Costa Navarino Golf](https://www.costanavarino.com/golf/)

**Camiral (ex-PGA Catalunya)**
- Online booking portal on a subdomain, with separate "areas" for tee times (golf.camiral.com/camiral/consumer/book?area=1) and **lessons** (…?lang=en&area=2) — [Camiral](https://www.camiral.com/en)

### Inferences
- Camiral's booking URL structure (/consumer/book) matches Golfmanager's (/consumer/ebookings), so Camiral probably also runs on Golfmanager, white-labelled on its own subdomain. This is unconfirmed. If so, the same engine serves both tee times and academy lessons, a model CamSur could copy by adding a "Lessons" resource type to its existing booking flow.
- Pay-in-full at booking (Gleneagles visitors) is the norm at the top end. CamSur's downpayment model is more lenient than these peers, which could be a selling point. A published cancellation policy would build trust, since none of these sites makes one easy to find.
- A free, no-deposit ballot or lottery suits only extreme-demand venues. A small resort could still borrow the mechanics cheaply for peak dates or tournaments, such as a "request a tee time / waitlist with SMS notification".
- Showing guest vs visitor prices side by side, as Gleneagles does, is a simple, strong nudge to book a villa.

### Gaps
- Official St Andrews ballot, cancellation and caddie pages could not be read (the fetch returned only navigation). The handicap limit (36 vs 24/36), entry channels and notification time all conflict between media sources.
- No cancellation or deposit policy was found for Gleneagles, Adare, Costa Navarino or Camiral on the pages fetched.
- No green fees were retrieved for Costa Navarino or Camiral, whose fees sit inside the booking engines or on pages not fetched.
- No evidence of dynamic pricing at any of the five. Gleneagles uses month-based seasonal pricing only.
- No advance booking window was found for Gleneagles, Costa Navarino or Camiral.

## Stay-and-play packages, lodging integration, multi-course packages

### Takeaway
All five link golf to rooms, but loosely. Packages are mostly marketing pages or offers routed to a separate hotel booking engine, not a single combined cart. Adare makes golf effectively resident-only, which forces the stay. Gleneagles and Camiral sell named, short packages (one night plus one round; two nights plus an experience).

### Cited Findings
- Gleneagles: "Tee It Up" (one hotel night plus one round on any of three championship courses), "Glorious Golf Breaks" page, corporate golf page — [Gleneagles Golf](https://gleneagles.com/golf/); [Green fees](https://gleneagles.com/golf/green-fees/)
- Adare Manor: golf is reserved for residents in 2026, so a green fee has to be paired with a hotel night. "Golf getaways" page at /experiences/golf-getaways/ — [Adare green fees](https://www.adaremanor.com/golf/our-course/green-fees/); [Adare Golf](https://www.adaremanor.com/golf/); [golfforkings via search](https://golfforkings.pages.dev/guides/how-to-play-adare-manor/)
- Costa Navarino: dedicated "Stay & Play Packages" page (costanavarino.com/stay-and-play-packages/) covering four courses (Dunes, Bay, Hills, International Olympic Academy) — [Costa Navarino Golf](https://www.costanavarino.com/golf/); [Golf resort](https://www.costanavarino.com/golf-resort/)
- Camiral: packages include a "Golf & Wellness" retreat offer, a "Road to 2031 two-night experience" (Superior Room plus breakfast at Hotel Camiral) and a "Winter warmer" (15% off accommodation). Three lodging types (Hotel Camiral, Lavida Hotel, Holiday Rentals) are booked through a separate engine at stay.camiral.com. Spa treatments are booked through yet another system (camiral.onejourney.travel) — [Camiral](https://www.camiral.com/en)
- St Andrews: the Swilcan Package bundles two rounds with F&B and retail credits — [Golf365](https://www.golf365.com/news/how-to-get-tee-time-st-andrews). The app's "Digital Wallet" (v1.5.0) holds gift cards, promotional offers and "Winter Package credit vouchers" — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/st-andrews-links-home-of-golf/id6670563713)

### Inferences
- Even top resorts run tee times, rooms and spa on separate systems (Camiral uses at least three booking domains). CamSur's integrated tee-time-plus-villa add-on is already ahead of these sites on unified checkout.
- Bundling resort credits (F&B and pro-shop credit, as in the Swilcan Package) into packages is a cheap way to add perceived value without discounting the green fee.
- Event-themed packages (Camiral's "Road to 2031" for the Ryder Cup) show how to tie a stay offer to a story or milestone.

### Gaps
- Package prices and inclusions for Costa Navarino, Gleneagles "Glorious Golf Breaks" and Adare "Golf getaways" were not retrieved.
- No evidence of multi-course passes (such as a 3-round pass) at Costa Navarino or Camiral on the pages read.

## Course information (flyovers, hole guides, live status, history/architect content)

### Takeaway
Architect and pedigree storytelling is universal: Braid and Nicklaus, Fazio, Langer, RTJ Jr. and Olazábal all feature. The most useful player-facing digital features are **live course status** (Gleneagles' web page) and **live pin positions plus a GPS course guide** (St Andrews app). Adare offers a **virtual course tour**.

### Cited Findings
- Gleneagles: course pages for the King's (James Braid), Queen's (par-68 heathland), PGA Centenary (Jack Nicklaus) and the 9-hole par-3 Wee Course. **Live course status and closure schedules are published on the golf page**, and the PGA Academy's opening hours that day ("8.00am–4.00pm… last balls 3.30pm") were shown, i.e. updated daily — [Gleneagles Golf](https://gleneagles.com/golf/)
- Adare Manor: course info (about 7,509 yds, par 72), a dedicated "Tom Fazio design" page (2016–17 rebuild), a **"Virtual course tour"** page (/golf/our-course/virtual-tour/), signature holes 15 and 18 highlighted, and a Ryder Cup 2027 page (13–19 Sep 2027). No hole-by-hole guide was found on the main golf page — [Adare Golf](https://www.adaremanor.com/golf/)
- Costa Navarino: one page per course. Dunes by Bernhard Langer with European Golf Design (6,581 yds); Bay by Robert Trent Jones Jr.; Hills (par 72, 6,280 yds) and International Olympic Academy course (par 72, 6,366 m) attributed to José María Olazábal (wording ambiguous) — [Costa Navarino golf resort](https://www.costanavarino.com/golf-resort/)
- Camiral: Stadium and Tour course pages, including a Tour-course "slope" sub-page. The Tour course has hosted the European Tour Q-School Final Stage seven times since 2008. There is a "Golf Hub" page — [Camiral](https://www.camiral.com/en)
- St Andrews app: "Course Guides", "Live Pin Positions", "View All Pin Locations" (v1.4.0), "GPS Course Guide" with a **lock-screen yardage Live Activity** (v1.2.0), and an "Old Course Reversed" overview (v1.3.0) — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/st-andrews-links-home-of-golf/id6670563713). The website has a "Live Webcam" (standrews.com/webcam) — [St Andrews](https://standrews.com/golf/links-ticketholders)
- The 2019 app update already included live weather, course announcements and shuttle bus times between venues — [Golf Business News, 7 May 2019](https://golfbusinessnews.com/news/corporate/st-andrews-links-unveils-app-update). This is an older version; the current app was relaunched in 2025.

### Inferences
- CamSur already has hole-by-hole pages. Cheap upgrades seen here are a daily "course status" banner (open/closed, cart rules, range hours) and a published daily pin sheet, both editable by staff in an admin panel.
- A webcam (St Andrews) or a virtual tour (Adare) are high-trust, low-cost content.
- A "slope/rating" page (Camiral) helps handicap-aware visitors.

### Gaps
- Could not confirm whether any of the five offer drone flyover videos per hole. Adare's virtual-tour page content was not fetched.
- No live course status feature was found for Adare, Costa Navarino or Camiral on the pages read.

## Player services (academies, club hire, practice, fitting, shop, vouchers, apps, loyalty, membership, WHS)

### Takeaway
Every resort pairs golf with a named academy (Gleneagles and Camiral both host "PGA National Academy" brands), premium club hire with public prices, an online shop and gift vouchers. St Andrews' 2025 app is the benchmark for mobile: ballot, booking, wallet, GPS, pins, handicap calculator and restaurant reservations. Costa Navarino has a resort-wide app. None showed explicit WHS/handicap-system integration beyond St Andrews' handicap calculator.

### Cited Findings
- **St Andrews app** ("St Andrews Links: Home of Golf", St Andrews Links Trust, free; initial version 27 May 2025, latest v1.5.1 on 20 Oct). Features: Enter the Old Course Ballot, Tee Time Booking (Ticketholders), Manage Reservations, Course Guides, Live Pin Positions, Notifications, Explore, Latest News, **Busy Dates**, Feedback. Release notes add a Handicap Calculator (v1.1.0, Sep 2025), Native Golf Booking, a lock-screen yardage Live Activity, Personalised News (v1.2.0), save tee time to calendar, **restaurant table reservations**, events/offers (v1.3.0), "Saved Golfers", Profile, "Golf Services" (v1.4.0), and a **Digital Wallet** for gift cards, promos and package credit vouchers, plus Green Fee Information and Information Banners (v1.5.0). It is rated 5.0 from only 4 ratings — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/st-andrews-links-home-of-golf/id6670563713)
- Gleneagles: PGA National Golf Academy with its own page (gleneagles.com/golf/pga-academy/); a complimentary range basket is included. The Bag Store has 80 rental sets (Titleist, Callaway, TaylorMade, Ping, Mizuno). Published hire prices include clubs £55/round, £70 with trolley and 3 balls, £80/day, £210/week, Powakaddy £28 and shoes £25. Also: membership (access to all three championship courses, the clubhouse and member events), an online shop (shop.gleneagles.com) and gift vouchers (vouchers.gleneagles.com) — [Golf](https://gleneagles.com/golf/); [Golfer's journey](https://gleneagles.com/golf/golfers-journey/); [Green fees](https://gleneagles.com/golf/green-fees/)
- Adare Manor: Pro Shop with GPS carts, premium club hire and electric trolleys; a **drinks buggy service** during rounds; an online pro shop (shop.adaremanor.com/collections/golf-pro-shop) with Ryder Cup × Adare Manor collections; gift vouchers; driving range, short-game area and putting green; "Adare Manor Golf Membership" page (no details); lessons €105 / 60 min — [Adare Golf](https://www.adaremanor.com/golf/); [Green fees](https://www.adaremanor.com/golf/our-course/green-fees/)
- Costa Navarino: Navarino Golf Academy (individual and small-group lessons "using current technology"), Practice and Facilities & Equipment pages, and a resort app for iOS/Android — [Golf resort](https://www.costanavarino.com/golf-resort/); [Golf](https://www.costanavarino.com/golf/)
- Camiral: PGA National Academy ("Spain's National PGA Academy"), lessons bookable online in the same booking portal as tee times, a "Golf services" page and a real-estate "Residents Club" — [Camiral](https://www.camiral.com/en)

### Inferences
- The St Andrews "Busy Dates" feature (showing which dates are in high demand) and its "Digital Wallet" for vouchers and credits are distinctive, and either could be built as a simple web feature. CamSur would not need a native app; a PWA would do.
- Publishing a full rental price list, as Gleneagles and Adare do, removes friction for fly-in golfers. Online gift vouchers on a separate subdomain are standard at all three UK/Irish venues.

### Gaps
- No custom-fitting services were confirmed at any venue on the pages read.
- No loyalty programmes were found.
- No "international membership" products were found. Gleneagles and Adare list membership pages, but no prices or international tiers were retrieved.
- No details were found on Costa Navarino's app features, such as tee booking or GPS.
- No explicit WHS / national handicap-system integration was found. St Andrews' 2019 app let users store proof of handicap via a phone photo ([Golf Business News 2019](https://golfbusinessnews.com/news/corporate/st-andrews-links-unveils-app-update)), but whether that survives in the 2025 app is unconfirmed.
- No Camiral academy prices or Costa Navarino lesson prices were found.

## Content and trust (storytelling, events, webcams, live chat, languages, sustainability)

### Takeaway
Heritage and major-event storytelling dominate: Ryder Cup 2027 at Adare, Ryder Cup 2031 at Camiral, the Home of Golf, PGA pro-ams. Multi-language sites appear mainly in continental Europe (Camiral in five languages; Costa Navarino newsletter in five). Sustainability is formalised mostly at Costa Navarino.

### Cited Findings
- St Andrews: "The Home of Golf" content hub and a Live Webcam; email sign-up for "tee times, career opportunities and more" — [St Andrews](https://standrews.com/golf/links-ticketholders)
- Adare Manor: Ryder Cup 2027 page, tournaments page (Irish Open, J.P. McManus Pro-Am history), dining reservations through TablePath — [Adare Golf](https://www.adaremanor.com/golf/)
- Camiral: site in **English, Spanish, Catalan, German, French**; Ryder Cup 2031 page; tournaments and corporate events pages; a brand-history page explaining the rebrand — [Camiral](https://www.camiral.com/en)
- Costa Navarino: Aegean Messinia Pro-Am (20th year) and Legends Tour (3rd edition) events; an online 2026 magazine (fliphtml5); newsletter in Greek, English, German, French and Russian; a sustainability page and an environmental policy PDF uploaded Jul 2026 — [Golf resort](https://www.costanavarino.com/golf-resort/); [Golf](https://www.costanavarino.com/golf/)

### Inferences
- A digital magazine or story hub (Costa Navarino, St Andrews) and a "road to [event]" narrative (Camiral) are cheap content patterns. CamSur could run a "Road to [local tournament]" series or a seasonal e-magazine.
- Offering languages that match the visitor source markets (Camiral's choice of DE and FR) is the right model. For CamSur that likely means EN, Tagalog/Bikol, Korean, Japanese and Chinese.

### Gaps
- Live chat was not observed on any of the pages fetched; it may exist as a script widget the fetcher does not capture.
- Language options for the Gleneagles, Adare and St Andrews sites (they appear English-only) were not verified.
- Course-level sustainability details (water, grass, GEO certification) were not retrieved.

## Distinctive or innovative ideas a smaller resort could copy cheaply

### Takeaway
The best ideas are operational transparency (live status, busy dates, pin sheets), fair-access mechanics (lottery or draw with SMS notification), wallet and credit bundling, and visible guest-vs-visitor pricing. All of these are buildable on CamSur's existing Next.js + Turso stack without new hardware.

### Cited Findings
- "Busy Dates" in-app calendar and "Information Banners" (St Andrews) — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/st-andrews-links-home-of-golf/id6670563713)
- Singles draw with email/SMS notification and pairing into incomplete groups (St Andrews) — [Top100](https://www.top100golfcourses.com/golf-travel/how-the-st-andrews-old-course-ballot-actually-works-everything-you-need-to-know)
- Package credits (F&B £65, retail £25) bundled into the Swilcan Package — [Golf365](https://www.golf365.com/news/how-to-get-tee-time-st-andrews)
- Digital Wallet for gift cards, promos and package vouchers (St Andrews v1.5.0) — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/st-andrews-links-home-of-golf/id6670563713)
- Live course status, closure schedule and daily academy hours on the golf page (Gleneagles) — [Gleneagles Golf](https://gleneagles.com/golf/)
- Group-of-four discount on off-peak weekdays ("Tee Time offer", from £160pp) (Gleneagles) — [Green fees](https://gleneagles.com/golf/green-fees/)
- Drinks buggy service, and a forecaddie option for two golfers sharing a cart (Adare) — [Adare green fees](https://www.adaremanor.com/golf/our-course/green-fees/)
- Lessons bookable in the same engine as tee times (Camiral) — [Camiral](https://www.camiral.com/en)
- Low-price access initiative to grow the game: Old Course rounds at £42.50 under "The Drive" (St Andrews) — [Golf365](https://www.golf365.com/news/how-to-get-tee-time-st-andrews)

### Inferences
Mapped to CamSur's current feature set, ranked by effort:
- Admin-editable "Today at the course" banner showing open/closed, cart path only, range hours and pin positions. Low effort.
- "Busy dates" heatmap on the booking date picker, drawn from existing reservation data. Low effort.
- Guest-vs-visitor price toggle on the green-fee page to nudge villa bookings. Low effort.
- Lessons and club hire as add-ons in the existing booking flow, with a published rental price list. Medium effort.
- Waitlist or "request a slot" for fully booked days with SMS notification. Medium effort.
- Member/guest wallet for voucher codes and package credits (F&B, pro-shop) redeemed at checkout. Medium effort.
- Off-peak foursome discount rule. Low effort.
- Webcam or virtual tour embed. Low effort.
- Community-access "local rate" day. Low effort.

### Gaps
- No published conversion or usage data was found showing how much these features lift bookings.
- "The Drive" initiative details (eligibility, how to apply) were not found on an official source.
