OUR STORY
Championship golf,
beneath Mt. Isarog.
In San Jose, Pili, on 54.23 hectares of Bicol countryside, eighteen
championship holes unfold toward the broad shoulders of Mt. Isarog.
CamSur Uptown Golf was created to give Camarines Sur a club of genuine
international standard — and to do it in a way that could only happen here.
We are a place of unhurried mornings, honest golf and gracious evenings.
Our brand borrows its calm from the mountain, its warmth from Bicolano
hospitality and its discipline from the game itself.

THE MASTER LOGO
One crest,
one name.
The CamSur Uptown Golf logo pairs the pili-shaped shield with an inscriptional
serif wordmark: CAMSUR UPTOWN GOLF, finished with “Est. 2026” between
two fine rules.
This full-colour vertical arrangement is the primary logo and should be the first
choice wherever space allows — signage, print, merchandise, digital headers
and social avatars.
Never re-typeset the wordmark. The lettering is part of the artwork; use the
supplied PNG / vector files only.

## Page 13

i.
ORIGIN · PILI NUT
The silhouette is a pili nut
Pili, the club’s home town, is named after Canarium
ovatum, Bicol’s signature nut. Its hard shell gives the shield
its outline: a pointed apex, broad shoulders and a softly
pointed base.
The form is symmetrical on a vertical axis and widest at 45 %
of its height, so it reads as a protective crest and a seed of
growth at the same time.
Ratio  width : height = 5 : 7 (10x : 14x).

ORIGIN · MT. ISAROG
The summit is Mt. Isarog
The dormant stratovolcano that dominates the
Camarines Sur skyline, seen from almost every hole. It is
drawn as a single gold ridge with a broken crater notch,
set just left of centre.
The flagstick rises to its right, taller than the summit,
putting the game in front of the landscape.
Placement  summit 5.1x below the apex; flag 6.25x from

## Page 16

CONSTRUCTION
Construction grid
Unit  x = shield width ÷ 10. Every measurement in this system is a
multiple of x, so the mark scales without drift.
Envelope  10x wide × 14x tall (5 : 7). Vertical axis of symmetry; widest
at 6.3x from the apex.
Frame  Gold border 0.36x; emerald channel 0.5x; inner gold rule
0.15x.
Landmarks  Summit 5.1x down at 4.65x across; flagstick 6.25x
across, from 4.3x to 8.1x down.
Never  rebuild the shield from this grid. The grid is for checking
reproductions, signage and embroidery digitising against the
master file.

## Page 17

LOCKUP GEOMETRY
Master logo proportions
Overall  12x wide × 22.7x tall. The wordmark (12x) is slightly wider than the shield
(10x), which anchors the crest visually.
Vertical rhythm  shield 14x · space 1x · CAMSUR 1.65x · 0.65x · UPTOWN 1.65x · 0.75x ·
GOLF 1.25x · 0.9x · Est. 2026 0.75x.
Hierarchy  CAMSUR and UPTOWN share one cap height; GOLF is set at 75 % of it;

Lettering  Classical inscriptional capitals, part of the artwork. Never re-typeset; for
live text alongside, use Cormorant Garamond.

CLEAR SPACE
Give the logo room
to breathe
Clear space is defined by x — the cap height of the word “CAMSUR”
in the wordmark.
Keep at least 1x clear of text, images, edges and other logos on
every side. On premium print and signage, use 2x.
When the shield is used alone, x is one-fifth of the shield’s width.

## Page 26

LOGO ON PHOTOGRAPHY
Placing the logo
on images
Place the logo over calm areas of sky, mist, water or turf —
never across faces, horizons or busy foliage.
Use the full-colour logo on mid-to-dark images and the
light-background version on pale, airy images.
Where the background is uneven, add a soft Onyx or
Uptown Emerald overlay (20–40 %) rather than a box
behind the logo.
Preferred positions: top-centre for portrait formats; left third
for landscape.
