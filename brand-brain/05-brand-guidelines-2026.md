## 3. Voice [BG p.8, 33]

- **Calm:** short sentences, room to breathe; ideally no exclamation marks.
- **Evocative:** lead with feeling and place (light, mist, the first drive); facts follow to reassure.
- **Courteous:** invite, don't push. "Reserve your round", not "Book now!!".
- **We write:** "The first tee is waiting." · "Wake slowly. Play when the light is kind." · "An evening that is yours." · "Reserve your round."
- **We avoid:** shouting, "cheap rates, hurry", slang, memes, emoji strings, unverified superlatives ("world’s #1").
- **Headlines:** sentence case; italics for emotional emphasis [BG p.32].
- **Spelling:** British throughout (colour, centre, travelled, enquire, digitise). Applied to the site and app on 2026-11-03.
- **Serial comma:** not used ("villas, cabins, cabanas and Dwell suites"). The site still mostly uses it: **owner call** on a sitewide change.
- **Proper names:** Members’ Lounge, Sky Bar, Villa Del Rey, Stay & Play.

**CTAs used in the guidelines:** Reserve your round · Plan your round · Plan your event · Find your stay · See packages · Book a fourball · Enquire · RSVP · Reserve.

## 4. IMG credit [BG p.38–43]

- Wording: "Course design by IMG" (Montserrat caps) or "An IMG-designed championship course" (in copy).
- Credit the course design only. Never imply IMG owns or operates the club.
- IMG logo only with IMG's written approval, at equal weight, 2x clear space, never merged with the shield. **Owner confirmed approval exists (2026-11-03).**
- Background facts (for press, not headlines): IMG Golf Course Services, 30+ years, 100+ courses; in the Philippines Eagle Ridge (Cavite), The Cliffs at Poro Point, the Wack Wack West redesign.
- Design philosophy: "18 broad fairway corridors follow the centre parcel of the site, one journey from the opening tee to the home green, with open views of Mt. Isarog." · "Seven water features shape the strategy while keeping the landscape open." · Playability for every skill level · eco-conscious approach to turf and water.

## 5. Logo [BG p.10–26]

- Master logo: pili-shaped shield + inscriptional serif wordmark CAMSUR UPTOWN GOLF + "Est. 2026" between fine rules. Never redraw, retype or rearrange; use master PNG/SVG only.
- Shield anatomy: pili-nut outline (Pili, the home town), Pili Gold border, Mt. Isarog in gold, flagstick, winding fairway lines, Uptown Emerald field with a Fairway Moss band.
- Versions: full colour (gold on emerald/onyx/dark photos) · light background (emerald wordmark) · shield alone · wordmark alone · gold foil outline · one colour (onyx, emerald or white).
- Clear space: x = cap height of "CAMSUR"; 1x minimum, 2x on premium print and signage. Shield alone: x = one-fifth of shield width.
- Minimum sizes (digital): master 120 px wide · shield 48 px high · outline icon 24 px · wordmark 140 px. Favicon uses the outline shield; app icon is the shield on emerald leather, never the full lockup.
- On photos: calm sky, mist, water or turf; never across faces or horizons; 20–40 % Onyx or Emerald overlay instead of a box.
- A vector redraw of the master is still needed before signage, foil or embroidery [BG p.24].

## 7. Typography [BG p.32–33]

- Cormorant Garamond: headlines, taglines, pull quotes, menus, numbers in the app. Sentence case.
- Montserrat: body, captions, buttons, navigation; eyebrows in bold tracked caps (+200–300).
- Scale: eyebrow Montserrat Bold caps · display Cormorant 64–96 pt · headline Cormorant 32–48 pt · subhead Cormorant Bold 18–24 pt · body Montserrat 9–11 pt, 140 % leading · caption Montserrat 7–8 pt.
- **Website migrated 2026-11-03:** headings Cormorant Garamond (was Cormorant SC small caps), body Montserrat (was Manrope). The Expo app still uses its own fonts.

## 8. Photography, clubhouse, campaign, app, merchandise (summary)

- **Photography [BG p.36–37]:** golden and blue hour, mist, long shadows; Mt. Isarog and palms in frame; a third of the frame left calm for type; natural grade, no HDR. People candid, faces often turned; dress code visible; no stock smiles or competitor brands.
- **Clubhouse [BG p.44–50]:** brand-application notes per space. Copy now used on the site: Members’ Lounge "Facing a full sweep of glass across the lake and fairways, a calm room for coffee, papers and post-round conversation." · Practice & play "Wake slowly, warm up with the lake in view, then head to the first tee."
- **Campaign [BG p.51–73]:** "The Uptown Life". Social posts: shield above, up to four words, one CTA; captions one sentence with #CamSurUptownGolf first. Handle @camsuruptowngolf; domain camsuruptowngolf.com ("swap in the final domain when it goes live").
- **Member App [BG p.74–80]:** **built 2026-11-03** in `mobile/` (replacing the website-clone app): leather sign-in, five tabs (Home, Book, Schedule, Club, Profile), booking confirmation, offer, member card with QR. Real: sign-in (`/api/app/session`, `/api/app/me`) and tee times. Sample data (`mobile/src/data/sample.ts`): events, schedule, dining, bays, wellness, offers, member number, handicap, guest passes. Dining/bay/wellness/membership send a call-back request. Tee slots follow the backend's 30-minute interval, not the PDF's 10. Spec and plan: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-03-member-app-design.md`, `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-11-03-member-app.md`.
- **Merchandise and cart [BG p.81–98]:** Fairway Line signature (one fine line per garment, always with a small shield); cart livery in gloss Uptown Emerald with gold pinstripe.
